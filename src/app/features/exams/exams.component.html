A theory paper only publishes once its uploaded question marks add up to the paper's total — that way a student can never sit a paper worth less than it claims. A practical carries no questions and publishes straight away.

Practical marks for an examination are entered here, on the practical paper's own mark sheet — not on the Marks screen. The Marks screen is the separate legacy record that feeds a student's statement and certificate; marks typed there never reach the examination portal, and the student's term grade stays Pending. Every examination needs both its papers before a student can be graded on it.
